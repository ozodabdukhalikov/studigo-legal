Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to STUDIGO ("STUDIGO", "we", "us", or "our"). STUDIGO is a digital platform designed to connect students with participating businesses and provide access to student discounts, benefits, promotional offers, and related services in Uzbekistan.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, disclosed, and protected when you use the STUDIGO mobile application and related services.

By using STUDIGO, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your account type and the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, phone number, and authentication information.
  • Student information: year of birth, educational institution or student status, verification status, student identification code, and profile photograph.
  • Business partner information: business name, contact information, business profile details, products, discounts, and offers.
  • QR and activity information: QR token identifiers, discount usage records, transaction amounts, savings, participating businesses, and timestamps.
  • Subscription information: selected membership plan, subscription status, and relevant billing records where applicable.
  • Referral and reward information: referral codes, referral relationships, reward points, and redemption history.
  • Technical information: device-related information, diagnostic data, security events, and application usage information where collected by our services.

3. How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure user accounts.
  • Verify student eligibility and account status.
  • Authenticate users and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Display participating businesses, products, and discounts.
  • Generate and validate student QR codes.
  • Record discount usage and calculate savings.
  • Manage subscriptions and membership benefits.
  • Administer referrals, reward points, and redemptions.
  • Provide customer support and service communications.
  • Detect fraud, misuse, and suspicious activity.
  • Maintain and improve application reliability and security.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Authentication and SMS Verification

STUDIGO may use email authentication and, where implemented, SMS verification to confirm account ownership, support sign-in, and protect accounts.

Phone numbers and verification-related information may be processed by authentication and messaging service providers for these purposes.

5. QR Codes and Discount Transactions

STUDIGO uses QR codes or student codes to help participating businesses verify eligibility for discounts and record qualifying transactions.

When a discount is used, relevant information such as the participating business, transaction amount, applied discount, savings, and time of the transaction may be recorded.

Authorized business representatives may access transaction information necessary to verify and manage transactions involving their business.

STUDIGO's QR transaction records are used for discount verification and activity tracking. Recording a transaction does not necessarily mean that STUDIGO processes the customer's payment.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

STUDIGO may offer STANDARD, PLUS, and PRO membership plans with different benefits.

Where paid subscriptions are available, payment processing may be performed by an authorized payment service provider or an applicable application marketplace.

STUDIGO may receive payment confirmation, subscription status, transaction identifiers, and billing-related information needed to manage membership access.

Payment card information is handled according to the practices of the applicable payment provider. We do not require users to provide full payment card details directly through STUDIGO's discount-tracking features.

7. Referrals and Reward Points

STUDIGO may process referral codes, account verification status, reward balances, and redemption records to operate its referral and rewards programs.

Information may also be used to detect duplicate referrals, fraudulent activity, or abuse of promotional benefits.

8. Service Providers and Data Storage

STUDIGO uses Supabase for backend services, including database functionality, authentication, and related infrastructure.

Information may be processed by Supabase and other service providers that support application hosting, authentication, messaging, security, payment processing, or technical operations, where those services are used.

Service providers are expected to process information only as necessary for their authorized functions and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

9. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with:

  • Participating businesses, where necessary to verify discounts and manage transactions.
  • Technology providers supporting the operation and security of STUDIGO.
  • Payment providers, where payments or subscriptions are processed.
  • Public authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

STUDIGO does not sell personal information to third-party data brokers.

10. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

These measures may include authenticated access, role-based permissions, database access policies, and secure communications.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account functionality, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized unless continued retention is necessary for legitimate legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Certain transaction or financial records may need to be retained for legally required periods.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request:

  • Access to their personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of their account and associated data.
  • Information about how their data is processed.
  • Other rights provided by applicable law.

To submit a privacy request, contact: studigo.uz@gmail.com .

13. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their STUDIGO account and associated personal information.

Instructions are available on our Account Deletion page .

We may need to verify account ownership before completing a deletion request. Some information may be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate purposes.

14. Children's Privacy

STUDIGO is intended for eligible students and participating business representatives. Eligibility and age requirements may depend on the services and features offered.

Where the processing of a minor's information requires consent from a parent or legal guardian under applicable law, appropriate consent must be obtained before such processing.

15. International Data Processing

Some technology providers may process or store information outside Uzbekistan. Where international transfers occur, we seek to comply with applicable data protection requirements.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
