Delete Your Account

STUDIGO allows students and suppliers to request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal data directly through the application.

1. Delete Your Account in the App

You can submit an account deletion request directly from the STUDIGO mobile application.

  1. Open the STUDIGO application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Profile.
  4. Go to Settings.
  5. Find the Delete Account option.
  6. Read the information about account deletion.
  7. Confirm your deletion request.
No administrator approval is required. Account deletion requests submitted through the application are registered and processed automatically by the STUDIGO server. Deletion is not necessarily immediate.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account or the application, you may request deletion by contacting STUDIGO Support.

  1. Send an email to STUDIGO Support.
  2. Use the email address associated with your STUDIGO account, if available.
  3. Include your registered email address or phone number to help us locate your account.
  4. Our support team may request additional information to verify account ownership.
  5. Once verified, your request can be submitted for account deletion processing.

3. How Account Deletion Works

When a valid deletion request is submitted through the application, STUDIGO registers the request for automated server processing.

  • The request is recorded in the system.
  • The server processes eligible deletion requests.
  • Account and associated personal data are removed or anonymized according to the applicable deletion process.
  • After deletion is completed, the deleted account cannot be restored.

Submitting a request does not mean that deletion has already been completed.

4. Student Accounts

Student users can request deletion of their accounts directly from the application.

Once processed, deletion may result in permanent loss of profile information, student verification details, saved offers, referral benefits, reward points and account history.

5. Supplier Accounts

Suppliers can also request deletion of their STUDIGO accounts through the application.

However, supplier account deletion may be restricted when outstanding withdrawal requests or pending financial obligations exist.

Pending withdrawals

Supplier accounts with pending withdrawals cannot be deleted until the relevant withdrawal obligations have been resolved.

6. Administrative Account Deletion

Authorized STUDIGO administrators may initiate account deletion for Student and Supplier accounts through the administrative interface.

These requests are subject to server-side authorization and account eligibility checks. Administrators cannot use this function to delete other administrator accounts.

7. What Data Will Be Deleted?

Depending on the account type and the data associated with it, deletion may include:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Personal profile and contact details.
  • Student profile and verification information.
  • Profile photographs and student identifiers.
  • Referral information and reward balances.
  • Favorites and account preferences.
  • Other associated personal data that is no longer required.

8. What Data May Be Retained?

Certain records may be retained where necessary to comply with applicable laws, accounting obligations, fraud prevention, security requirements or dispute resolution.

This may include transaction records, payment-related information and records relating to outstanding financial obligations.

Where appropriate, retained records may be anonymized or separated from directly identifying account information.

Retained data will be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose and retained only for the applicable period.

9. Important Information

  • Account deletion is intended to be permanent.
  • Deleted accounts cannot be restored after processing is completed.
  • Deletion may result in loss of discounts, subscriptions, reward points, saved offers and other account benefits.
  • Uninstalling the STUDIGO application does not delete your account.
  • Signing out does not delete your account.
  • Email requests may require identity verification before processing.
Your privacy matters. For more information about the collection, processing and protection of personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.
