Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your STUDIGO account and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your STUDIGO account, please follow these steps:

  1. Click the "Request Account Deletion" button below.
  2. Send the email using the address associated with your STUDIGO account, if available.
  3. Include your STUDIGO account email address or registered phone number so that we can locate your account.
  4. Our support team will review your request and may contact you to verify account ownership.
  5. Once your identity has been verified, we will process your deletion request, subject to applicable legal requirements.
Request Account Deletion

Alternatively, send your request directly to: studigo.uz@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Depending on your account and the information associated with it, deletion may include:

  • Account profile and contact information.
  • Student profile and verification information.
  • Profile photographs and student identifiers.
  • Referral codes and associated reward information.
  • Favorites and account preferences.
  • Other personal information no longer required for legitimate or legal purposes.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain information may need to be retained when required by applicable law or necessary for legitimate accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

This may include relevant transaction records, financial records, or information needed to resolve outstanding obligations.

Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for the applicable purpose and retained only for the required period.

Important Information

  • Account deletion is intended to be permanent.
  • You may lose access to membership benefits, saved offers, reward points, and account history.
  • Uninstalling STUDIGO does not automatically delete your account or personal information.
  • We may request additional information to verify that you own the account.
Privacy matters. If you have questions about how your information is processed, please review our Privacy Policy or contact our support team.
